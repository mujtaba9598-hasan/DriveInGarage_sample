Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Tap any question below to expand detailed information regarding workshop pricing, turnarounds, and warranties.

How much does car repair cost?
Repair cost depends entirely on the required part and labor. We perform initial diagnostics and deliver a clear itemized quote upfront. Work begins only upon your formal approval with zero hidden charges.
How long does repair take?
Routine servicing such as oil changes and brake replacements are completed within the same day. Major mechanical or transmission overhauls typically require 1 to 3 business days, with regular status updates.
Do you provide diagnostics?
Yes. We operate multi brand computerized scanning systems that accurately read engine check codes, electrical sensor anomalies, transmission faults, and airbag status.
Do you offer pickup and drop off in Ras Al Khaimah?
Yes, vehicle recovery, pickup, and delivery services are available across all Ras Al Khaimah districts upon prior coordination through our WhatsApp hotline.
Which car brands do you service?
We service all major brands with deep specialization in American (Ford, Chevrolet, GMC, Dodge, Jeep), GCC regional models, European luxury vehicles, and Japanese brands (Toyota, Nissan, Lexus).
What warranty is provided on repairs?
All replacement parts installed by our technicians carry authentic manufacturer warranties, complemented by our workshop guarantee covering installation craftsmanship.
